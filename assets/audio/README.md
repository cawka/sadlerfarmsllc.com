# Film audio

How to generate, change and re-mix the narration and music is documented in
[`tools/narration/README.md`](../../tools/narration/README.md).

- `narration/` — approved voice clips and `narration.json` (script, timing, levels)
- `beds/` — 78 s music beds (Morning Mood, Fireflies and Stardust, Bama Country, Cattails)
- `soundtracks/` — finished mixes, packaged by `tools/build-media.sh film` as switchable audio
- `source/` — original music downloads (gitignored)

Licenses: Morning Mood (Musopen Symphony) is public domain; the three Kevin MacLeod
tracks (incompetech.com) are CC BY 4.0 and need the credit shown in the site footer.
