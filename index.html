The vision

One extraordinary landscape.

Sadler Farms is a rare opportunity to create a distinctive waterfront community on Lake Wheeler in Limestone County, Alabama — a place where rolling green hills, mature forests and expansive lake views come together.

The vision is a thoughtfully planned community of approximately 300 residences, from two-bedroom townhomes overlooking a common marina to private one-acre estate homesites along the water — designed around the land rather than on top of it.

Concept only — subject to planning, engineering and approvals.

An extraordinary waterfront setting

More than a mile of shoreline. Endless possibilities.

The property runs for more than a mile along the Tennessee River’s Wheeler Reservoir, just east of where the Elk River joins — partly direct waterfront and partly contiguous to vertical TVA bluff land — facing one of the widest stretches of the lake. Two protected coves cut into the shoreline, one suitable for a possible marina.

  • Boating and cruising the Tennessee River
  • Kayaking and paddleboarding along the shoreline
  • Fishing on Lake Wheeler
  • Swimming and watersports, where permitted

A common marina

The heart of the community.

Envision a marina nestled in a naturally protected cove — a gathering place for residents and the everyday gateway to the lake.

  • Common boat ramp
  • Shared dock for residents and guests
  • Individual dry boat storage
  • Landscaped gathering areas overlooking the water

Marina features, capacity and locations are subject to engineering, permitting and regulatory approval.

A landscape preserved for generations

More than a development. A legacy of land.

For more than 70 years, one family has cared for this property — its mature hardwoods, rolling hills and hidden meadows. The vision carries that forward:

  • Walking trails through mature forest and open fields
  • Common green space and scenic lake overlooks
  • Roads that follow the natural contours of the land
  • Homesites placed to preserve views and trees
